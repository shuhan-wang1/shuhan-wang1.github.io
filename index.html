04TRAJECTORY-CONDITIONED PREDICTION

Temporal Routing

What does an agent's partial trajectory really tell us?

My MSc dissertation studies compact trajectory-conditioned value estimation. Follow-up work distinguishes task difficulty from the ability to rank different attempts at the same task.

↳

Early aggregate discrimination can hide weak within-task discrimination. Later signals offer limited selection and pruning gains in the evaluated settings.

ModernBERTTemporal modellingEmpirical audits
MSc dissertation · UCL
05GEOMETRY & LLM QUANTISATION

RopeBand

A geometric optimum. An empirical limit.

Characterising the rotations that commute with RoPE and deriving a closed-form optimum for a scoped variance surrogate. Experiments test whether that structure helps dynamic 4-bit quantisation.

↳

The evaluated pairwise-only transforms did not beat full-head Hadamard. The study examines the mismatch between the surrogate, mixing support, and the quantiser's scale statistic.

RoPEW4A4KV4Theory + experiments
First author · TMLR under reviewPaper
06APPLIED AGENT SYSTEMS

RentCompass

From a research idea to a tool people can use.

An independently built UK rental agent that searches listings, plans commutes, and compares neighbourhoods. Structured state and tool-backed evidence connect conversation to practical decisions.

↳

A bounded function-calling loop, recoverable multi-turn state, evidence checks, optional MCP tools, and releases that can roll back.
